What haql-closeout does

haql-closeout is the period-level check that follows row-level QC. It asks whether the month ties together, then names the wells and days that stand in the way of a clean close.

In one sentence: It turns “everything looked fine” into a clean-or-blocked decision with evidence.

Production and operations

Protect the close

Find the contradictory well or field balance before the number is committed.

Partners and JV teams

Answer queries

Show that the period was reconciled, not merely summed.

Reporting teams

Carry a trail

Give every blocker a rule ID, a well, a day and a source row.

Why it matters

A period close is a commitment to partners, royalty owners or a filing. When a well test disagrees with the daily average or a field sells more than it produced, the contradiction usually surfaces on the last working day — after the number has already been sent.

  • A row-level pass can clear every record while the period still contradicts itself.
  • A well can be marked shut-in in one column and produce normally in another.
  • A field can deliver more than it produced; the summary may not expose it.
  • The person who locks the close is often not the person who has to defend it later.
Without haql-closeoutThe hidden problem

The period is locked because the summary looks plausible. A well test that disagrees with the daily average ships in the reporting package anyway.

With haql-closeoutThe visible answer

The blockers are named before the lock: the well, the day, the rule and the source row. The close becomes a decision backed by a report.

What it checks

The checks are period-level questions that a single-row review cannot answer.

Check areaWhat it flags
Well test vs daily averageA test rate that does not reconcile with reported daily volumes.
Downtime vs productionA well recorded shut-in while volumes say it was running.
Date continuityA reporting gap measured against that well's own cadence.
Field balanceVolumes sold or delivered exceeding what the field produced.
Cumulative continuityTotals that go backwards inside the period.
Duplicate well-daysThe same well reported twice for one date.
Signed closeoutAn optional attestation over the report and findings root.

Closeout is best run after haql-qc: QC clears the records, then closeout confirms that the period ties together.

What it does not do

A useful tool should be clear about its boundaries. These are the current limits, not hidden caveats.

  • It is a reconciliation check, not a full accounting close or an ERP posting.
  • It depends on the well-test, downtime and sales data you provide.
  • It does not generate regulator forms or make filing decisions.
  • It reports blockers; it does not decide whether a commercial disagreement is material.

Findings are advisory. The tool names a contradiction or a risk with the evidence behind it. The engineering, legal or commercial judgement remains with you.

Common questions

Is this the same as haql-qc?

No. QC asks whether a row is defensible; closeout asks whether the month ties together. They are designed to be used together.

Does it need an accounting system connection?

No. It works from exports and files, not a database or ERP integration.

What does “blocked” mean?

A blocker is a contradiction that needs a decision before the period can be signed off. It is not automatically an error; it is something a person must resolve.

What is a well test?

A measured flow rate taken while a well is tested. It should agree with the daily average reported for the same period.
