What haql-dataroom does

haql-dataroom is built for acquisition and divestiture (A&D) technical review. It reads the seller's package, maps the files to their roles and checks whether the production, well, regulatory, revenue, tax, reserve and log tables tell the same story.

In one sentence: It turns the seller's folder into a row-traced question list for the deal team.

Why it matters

The contradictions that move a price are spread across files. None is visible in a single table. Each takes an analyst cross-referencing two exports by hand — exactly the work that runs out of time before the bid.

  • A producing well can be missing from the well master.
  • Internal production can disagree with the RRC-reported lease volume.
  • A plugged or inactive well can still report production.
  • A claimed remaining volume can be unsupported by the history behind it.
Without haql-dataroomThe hidden problem

An analyst spot-checks production and the reserve summary, then moves on. Cross-table contradictions are found after signing, or not at all.

With haql-dataroomThe visible answer

The contradictions are named before the bid, each traced to a row, and become the question list for the seller.

What it checks

The checks compare tables that are normally reviewed in isolation.

Check areaWhat it flags
Orphan and barren wellsProduction without a well record, or a well with no production history.
RRC varianceInternal production that disagrees with the reported lease volume.
Interest and API identityOwnership lines that violate the revenue-interest identity, or malformed API numbers.
Duplicates and statusThe same well appearing twice, or a plugged/inactive well still producing.
Shut-in and timingDays-on and volume contradictions, or production dated before completion.
LAS and revenue linksLog headers that do not match, or revenue referencing a missing lease.
Severance and declineSeverance-taxable volume versus produced volume; claimed reserves unsupported by history.
Revenue internal valueA statement whose volume, price, deductions and value do not agree.

The full rule list and rule identifiers are shown in the report and on the product page.

What it does not do

A useful tool should be clear about its boundaries. These are the current limits, not hidden caveats.

  • It is not a valuation, reserve certification or investment recommendation.
  • It depends on the completeness of the seller's package; missing files limit what can be checked.
  • It does not run a data-room portal, upload service or multi-user deal workspace.
  • Findings are advisory: a contradiction is a question to resolve, not a conclusion about price.

Findings are advisory. The tool names a contradiction or a risk with the evidence behind it. The engineering, legal or commercial judgement remains with you.

Common questions

Does it replace technical due diligence?

No. It makes the cross-file review exhaustive and reproducible, but a qualified reviewer still interprets the findings and the asset.

Do I have to upload the dataroom?

No. The tool runs locally on the files you provide.

Does it value the asset?

No. It finds contradictions in the package; valuation and reserve judgement are separate.

What is a dataroom?

The folder of technical and commercial files a seller provides during an acquisition review.
