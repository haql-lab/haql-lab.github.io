What haql-45q does

The US 45Q tax credit supports carbon capture and storage (CCS). A claim needs more than a total: it needs a defensible mass balance, a consistent storage formation, complete reporting periods and the correct rate basis. haql-45q checks those links before a reviewer does.

In one sentence: It turns a credit calculation into a reviewed, traceable evidence pack.

Why it matters

Calculating a dollar value per tonne is arithmetic. Surviving review is harder: a vented tonne that never reached the balance, an injection row outside the storage formation, or a missing monitoring quarter can undermine the claim.

  • A vent or leak recorded in monitoring can be left out of the stored-tonne calculation.
  • Injection can be attributed to the wrong formation or interval.
  • A missing monitoring or lab quarter makes the year incomplete before it is filed.
  • The wrong construction-year rate table can restate the entire schedule.
Without haql-45qThe hidden problem

What it does not do

A useful tool should be clear about its boundaries. These are the current limits, not hidden caveats.

  • It does not provide legal, tax or accounting advice; it produces evidence for a professional to review.
  • It depends on the completeness and accuracy of the capture, injection, monitoring and lab records you provide.
  • It does not integrate with an accounting or EHS platform; files are the interface.
  • The facility configuration — formation, dates, disposition and MRV plan — must reflect the actual project.

Findings are advisory. The tool names a contradiction or a risk with the evidence behind it. The engineering, legal or commercial judgement remains with you.

Common questions

Is this a tax-return or legal-opinion tool?

No. It reconciles the technical and documentary evidence behind a claim. Legal, tax and accounting judgements belong to the relevant professionals.

Does it support pre-IRA projects?

The rate schedule uses the statutory table for the construction year; a pre-IRA project is not silently priced at the full current rate.

What happens when a file is missing?

Missing files are treated as gaps and reported, not guessed around. A missing required column is an error.

What is 45Q?

A US tax credit for carbon capture and storage. The credit depends on proving how much CO2 was stored and under what rules. This tool builds the technical evidence, not the tax return.

What is Subpart RR?

The US EPA reporting rule for geologic sequestration. It sets the annual reporting clock and the storage-site evidence a claim should line up with.
