haql-engine · deck, schedule and driver

The deck is the input. Now the schedule is too.

The property layer, static grid, well schedule and a generic time-step driver are now deck-driven. The standard SPE9 model builds as 9,000 cells, 26 wells and 900 days. A small synthetic deck runs end to end with a closed mass ledger. The full SPE9 run is deliberately not claimed as validated yet.

What changed

Before this increment, blackoil_vs.PVT() carried the SPE1 PVT and relative-permeability tables as module constants, and the only production driver was the SPE1 CASE1 replay. Both boundaries are now explicit:

Deck keywordNow used for
PVTOSaturated Rs, Bo, mu_o and undersaturated Bo(p,Rs), mu_o(p,Rs)
PVDG / PVTWBg(p), mu_g(p), Bw(p), mu_w(p)
SWOF / SGOFThree-phase water/gas/oil relative permeability
DENSITY / ROCKSurface densities, gravity and rock compressibility
WELSPECS / COMPDATWell locations, reference depth and multi-completion intervals
WCONPROD / WCONINJERate and BHP controls, wildcard well names, producer/injector pressure limits
TSTEPThe deck's own report-step sequence

PVT() still gives the historical SPE1 tables. A regression test asserts that the SPE1 deck tables are bit-identical to those defaults, so existing SPE1 results do not move.

Deck reading is broader, but still bounded. deckparse.py resolves recursive INCLUDE files relative to the including deck, detects include cycles, expands N*value and N*, and applies COPY/MULTIPLY array operations, including optional six-index I/J/K regions.

From static grid to a deck model

The standard SPE9 deck is Cartesian: 24 × 25 × 15 = 9,000 cells. Its published grid uses DX, DY, DZ, TOPS and PERMX, not corner-point COORD/ZCORN. Starting with corner-point geometry would add geometric risk before the property and schedule layers are deck-driven.

The model builder now closes the static and schedule part: EQUIL/RSVD initial state, 25 producers with three completions each, one water injector with five completions, wildcard WCONPROD records, and 90 TSTEP entries. run_deck.py is a generic driver over that model, not an SPE9-only wrapper.

Unsupported features still fail at the boundary. The builder raises ModelError for non-FIELD units, corner-point geometry, ACTNUM, faults, multiple regions, DRSDT, DATES, WCONHIST, WELTARG, WELOPEN, WELSHUT and group controls. Missing tables never fall back to SPE1 properties.

How to use it

from deckmodel import build_model
from run_deck import simulate

model = build_model("benchmark/decks/spe9/SPE9.DATA")
print(model.grid.n)        # 9000
print(len(model.wells))    # 26
print(len(model.steps))    # 90
print(model.total_days)    # 900.0

# A small synthetic deck; the standard SPE9 run is not yet validated.
model = build_model("fixtures/mini_blackoil.DATA")
out = simulate(model, T=3.0, dtmax=1.0, practical_tol=2e-4)
print(out["status"], out["t_reached"], out["mb"])

The driver can also be invoked directly:

cd engine
python3 run_deck.py --deck fixtures/mini_blackoil.DATA --T 3 --dtmax 1 \
    --practical-tol 2e-4
python3 run_deck.py --deck benchmark/decks/spe9/SPE9.DATA --T 30

The table builder accepts optional undersaturated PVTO continuations in the usual p Bo mu_o form. PVT validates monotonic pressures and saturations, positive densities and positive water properties, and reports a clear error instead of falling back to SPE1 values.

What the generic driver does

  • Applies each WCONPROD/WCONINJE record at its position in the deck's event stream.
  • Shares one BHP unknown across all completions of a well; the rate constraint sums the per-completion rates.
  • Substeps to dtmax; the solver retries a failed Newton step at a smaller time step.
  • Can switch a rate-controlled well to its declared BHP limit after a converged step. The switch is explicit and visible in the next record.
  • Records a signed conservation ledger from the same well terms the residual uses; drift is reported, not fabricated from rates alone.
  • Writes run summary JSON and optional field snapshots through the existing field exporter.

Supported schedule subset: WELSPECS, COMPDAT (OPEN only), WCONPROD ORAT/RATE/BHP, WCONINJE RATE/BHP, wildcard names and TSTEP. Group controls, shut-in events and historical controls are rejected.

SPE9 model and status

fieldgrid.build_cartesian assembles and validates the standard SPE9 geometry:

  • 24 × 25 × 15 cells, 9,000 active cells;
  • 300 ft in x and y;
  • the 15 published layer thicknesses from 20 ft to 100 ft;
  • TOPS per column, including the 10-degree x-direction dip;
  • PERMX, PERMY, PERMZ, PORO and TOPS shape, finiteness and positivity checks.

The SPE9 include file copies PERMX to PERMY and PERMZ, then multiplies PERMZ by 0.01. The test suite asserts that result, not just that a parser returned a number.

Model-build check, not a validated SPE9 run. The standard deck builds a 9,000-cell model with 25 producers, one injector, 90 steps and 900 days. The first production Newton step currently stalls near a scaled residual of 5e-3 around free/dissolved switching cells. No comparison against benchmark/references/SPE9/ is claimed. The synthetic fixtures/mini_blackoil.DATA deck completes three days with a finite ledger and is the current end-to-end regression.

How to check it

cd engine
python3 -m unittest -v test_deck_tables
python3 -m unittest -v test_provenance test_forecast test_geology test_fields
python3 selftest.py

test_deck_tables.py covers include expansion, missing/cyclic includes, COPY/MULTIPLY, the SPE9 grid, SPE9 PVT/relperm values, rejection of non-monotonic tables, the SPE1 default-equivalence check, the synthetic end-to-end driver, multi-completion wells, schedule controls, BHP-control parsing and rejection of unsupported DRSDT. The full engine suite remains green at 92 tests; the selftest remains 11/11.

Limits you should state

  • Full SPE9 time-stepping is not validated. The grid, tables, 26 wells and 900-day schedule build; the full nonlinear run and reference comparison remain open. This page does not claim an SPE9 history match.
  • No DRSDT. DRSDT is rejected rather than ignored. The historical SPE1 replay keeps its separate frozen-Rs DRSDT=0 path.
  • No corner-point geometry. COORD, ZCORN, ACTNUM, faults and EGRID-based full-field grids remain future work.
  • One table per keyword, one region. Multiple PVT/rock/saturation regions and EQUALS are not dispatched yet.
  • No group or historical controls. Group controls, shut-ins, DATES, WCONHIST, WELTARG, WELOPEN and WELSHUT are rejected.
  • Piecewise-linear interpolation. Derivatives are one-sided at exact table knots, the same convention as the SPE1 path.
